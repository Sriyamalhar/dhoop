    How a society uses Dhoop

    Someone on the committee signs up, adds the roof and the bills, and invites the treasurer and the installer. From there Dhoop keeps everybody working from the same numbers.

    Timings below are typical for a mid-sized society.

    1. Day 1

      Measure

      Add your roof area and twelve months of bills. Dhoop sizes the plant and shows the payback.

    2. Weeks 1 to 3

      Approve

      Dhoop drafts the resolution for your general body and tracks the net-metering and subsidy applications.

    3. Weeks 4 to 8

      Switch on

      Your installer works through a shared checklist: install, inspection, meter swap, go-live.

    4. Every month

      Share the savings

      Generation is reconciled with the DISCOM statement. Each flat sees its credit and settles by UPI.

    Questions committees ask us

    Do we have to use your installer?

    Dhoop doesn't install anything. Bring any installer. They get a login to update the checklist, and you keep control of the numbers.

    How are savings split between flats?

    Common-area bills such as lifts, pumps and corridor lights are credited first. The remaining credit is shared by flat size or equally, whichever your general body approves.

    What if the roof is shared between towers?

    Add each tower as its own roof in the same workspace. The Society plan covers three roofs, and Campus has no limit.

    What happens to residents' data?

    Resident details stay inside your society's workspace and are visible only to the people you invite. Payments go through UPI, so Dhoop never stores card details.

    Is this a real product?

    It's a concept project with sample data, built as a front-end assignment. Figures on this page are illustrative, and the demo form doesn't send anything.
